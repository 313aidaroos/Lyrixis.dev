How it works

Four steps. No pipeline to build.

01

Upload

Drop in MP3, WAV, M4A or FLAC — one file or ten thousand. Drag, drop, or POST to the API.

02

Analyze

Transcription, forced alignment, language and dialect ID, section detection, explicit screening.

03

Understand

Review the synced view, correct anything the model got wrong. Every edit autosaves and is versioned.

04

Export

TTML, LRC, SRT, VTT, JSON, CSV, TXT — or pull it straight from the API into your own systems.

Output

One upload. Every layer.

A single track returns a complete intelligence package — not just a transcript.

Lyrics & timing

Full transcription, line separation, word-level and line-level timestamps, per-line confidence scores.

Song structure

Intro, verse, pre-chorus, chorus, bridge, outro and instrumental sections with start and end times.

Language & dialect

Primary language, additional languages, and dialect where confidence supports it. Never asserted when it isn't.

Translation

Line-aligned translation into supported languages, keeping timing intact for subtitle and lyric workflows.

Transliteration

Arabic script to Latin, Hangul to romanization, and other scripts where a standard mapping exists.

Explicit screening

Clean, explicit or possibly explicit — with the specific words and lines highlighted for your review.

Vocal map

Vocal, instrumental and silent regions. Voice changes flagged for duets and featured sections.

Metadata

Title, artist, album, ISRC, label, publisher, writers and credits — carried through every export.

Corrections

Edit any line. Every version is preserved, so your catalog only ever gets more accurate over time.

On credits: Lyrixis never claims songwriter, publisher or ownership information inferred from audio is authoritative. Authoritative credits come from your metadata, your distributor, or a verified rights-holder integration.

Developers

Music intelligence through one API.

Upload a track, get an ID, poll for the package. Asynchronous by design — no request ever waits on a model.

POST /v1/tracks
# upload audio, receive a track id immediately
curl -X POST https://api.lyrixis.com/v1/tracks \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $LYRIXIS_API_KEY" \
  -F "audio=@track.wav" \
  -F "title=Untitled" \
  -F "rights_confirmed=true"

→ { "track_id": "trx_123", "status": "queued" }

FAQ

Straight answers.

What exactly do I get for one song?

A full transcription with word-level and line-level timestamps, detected song structure, language and dialect identification, explicit content screening, a vocal/instrumental map, your metadata carried through, and exports in TXT, JSON, CSV, SRT, LRC, VTT and TTML. Every automated result carries a confidence score.

What do I get for $30?

Lyrixis All-Access is $30 a month (3,000 Ixis). It renews every month. Cancel anytime and it stays active until the end of the month you already paid for. Unlimited song lyrics is included. Lyric video downloads, translations, and Voices are coming soon. You can still pay $3 (300 Ixis) for one song.

Can I pay with a card?

Yes. Pay by card or with Ixis (100 Ixis = $1). Card checkout is not open yet. Add Ixis in the Apixis Wallet. The $3 song unlock works today.

How do I cancel?

Cancel anytime. All-Access stays active until the end of the paid month. Email awad@apixis.dev. There is no cancel button yet, because card checkout is not open.

What is Ixis?

Ixis is the balance in the Apixis Wallet. 100 Ixis equals $1. One song is 300 Ixis ($3). All-Access is 3,000 Ixis ($30) a month.

Is there a plan for labels?

Yes. Label / Enterprise has no fixed price. Use Contact sales on the pricing page (company, your name, work email, phone if you want, songs per month, team seats, and a message). It is saved and emailed to awad@apixis.dev. After approval, the owner invites the team. Those people get All-Access on that company's songs only.

How accurate is it on sung vocals?

Music transcription is genuinely harder than speech. That's why Lyrixis is built around confidence scoring and correction rather than pretending the first result is final. Low-confidence lines are flagged so you know exactly where to look, and every correction you make is saved and versioned.

Which languages and dialects are supported?

Arabic (including Yemeni, Gulf, Egyptian and Levantine varieties), English, Spanish, French, Turkish, Urdu, Hindi, Korean and more. Dialect is only reported when confidence supports it — if we aren't sure, we say so rather than guessing.

Can I correct mistakes?

Yes. Every line and word is editable in the synced view, and edits autosave. Previous versions are preserved so nothing is ever lost.

What formats can I export?

TXT, JSON, CSV, SRT, LRC, VTT and TTML. TTML is built for professional time-synchronized lyric workflows. More formats can be added on request.

Who owns the output?

You do. Lyrixis processes recordings you own or are authorized to process, and the resulting data belongs to you. Full terms are being finalized with counsel before general availability.

Can I use Lyrixis on songs I don't own?

No. Every upload requires confirmation that you own, control, or have permission to process the recording. Lyrixis is not a tool for pulling audio from streaming services, and accounts doing so will be terminated.

How is my audio stored?

In private storage with signed, expiring access URLs. Audio is never publicly accessible. Enterprise customers can request defined retention periods and secure deletion on completion.

What is "human verified"?

A planned optional service. Results are labelled by verification level — AI Generated, AI Reviewed, Human Verified, or Verified by Rights Holder — so you always know the provenance of a line. Human review is not yet available.

Can I process a whole catalog?

Yes. Drag in thousands of files or submit a batch job through the API. You'll see live completed, processing, queued and failed counts, and can retry failures individually.

What stage is Lyrixis at?

Early access. The engine and pricing are live for pilot customers and the self-serve dashboard and public API are rolling out to onboarded accounts first. Email Lyrixis@Apixis.dev and we'll run a sample of your tracks.
